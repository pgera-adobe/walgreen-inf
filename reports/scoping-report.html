6. Program findings, risks and open decisions

  • Scale is dominated by data-driven walgreens.com families. Over 99.8 % of the program's URLs (store-locator × service × city, store details, /q/ search, brand/facet lists) are generated pages; they become a small number of EDS template pages with API-fed blocks (W4), not documents. The authored surface to import is ≈ 1,000 pages across all four sites.
  • Two of four properties are AEM (blog, WAG) with named cmp-* components, so source-component → block mapping is direct and bulk import is highly automatable. walgreensspecialtyrx.com is an Astro v5 static site that reuses AEM-style cmp-* class names. walgreens.com mixes AEM-authored marketing pages (home, promotions, topic pages) with JSP/React commerce apps.
  • Recommended order: P1 specialty pilot (18 pages, proves the gated pipeline) → P2 blog (≈ 760 pages, 8 templates) → P3 walgreens.com authored content + WAG merge → P4 walgreens.com integrated dynamic → P5 retain/redirect/decommission.
  • Owner decisions needed: (1) blog target — keep blog.walgreens.com subdomain or move under walgreens.com/blog; (2) WAG merge path on walgreens.com (proposed /advertising/…), whether WAG keeps its own header/footer, and whether WAG pages should start carrying walgreens.com tags and OneTrust consent (they have none today); (3) commerce/catalog/store/loyalty API access for W4 blocks (endpoints, auth, CORS, caching) and whether EDS serves on the www.walgreens.com host; (4) tag ownership on the new hosts (Adobe Launch, Analytics CNAME, Target, OneTrust, Medallia, Dynatrace/mPulse, retail-media ad tags); (5) form endpoints — Salesforce Web-to-Lead (WAG), Pardot embed and the PHI-bearing patient contact form /ebm/sendmail/contactus (specialty), walgreens.com contact and pharmacy-complaint forms; (6) Spanish — an /es/ tree for the 13 walgreens.com Spanish pages and the fate of untranslated /es/ URLs, plus whether the blog’s 30 Spanish articles get an /es tree; (7) an Akamai / Cloudflare allow-list for the migration crawler; (8) fix the WAG home-page links to the development AEM author host.
  • Redirects: every excluded/duplicate URL (author-path mirrors, legacy vanity URLs, third-party demo mirrors, WAG→walgreens.com) needs a 301 in redirects.tsv; WAG's own source already 404s on 174 header links that point to walgreens.com paths on the WAG host.
  • Bot management: walgreens.com (Akamai) occasionally rejects automated renders (HTTP/2 protocol errors, 403 on account pages); walgreensspecialtyrx.com sits behind a Cloudflare managed challenge. Import and fidelity-gate runs need the same browser hardening used in this capture, or allow-listing of the migration crawler.
  • Capture status (final): specialty 18/18, advertising 17/17, blog all in-scope pages but one (a single article times out on the source), walgreens.com 767 of the 1,167-page live roster. The rest of the walgreens.com roster (≈ 400, mostly /store/c/ product, category and facet lists, plus signed-in account pages) returned Akamai 403 "Access Denied" to the automated browser even at concurrency 2 with stealth settings; after 187 facet lists were captured the family was not pushed further (named deviation D6). Every uncaptured page is still classified into its template; the CSV marks capture status per URL.

7. Method and evidence

  • Discovery: sitemap recursion → robots.txt → same-origin BFS (depth 3), normalized and junk-filtered per stardust extract (ia-extraction.md). Full inventories: stardust/inventory/*.urls.tsv.
  • Live capture: stardust extract/scripts/crawl.mjs --dynamics (Playwright render, full-page screenshot, settled DOM, per-page dynamic signals). No page record was synthesized.
  • Page typing and template grouping: URL rules authored from captured evidence (stardust/inventory/*.rules.json); block mapping from component census of the rendered DOM (*.census.json, *.block-map.json).
  • Dynamic surface: stardust dynamics detect → classify → triage draft on one archetype per template.
  • Named deviations from the shipped skills are listed in stardust/direction.md; plugin feedback in stardust/plugin-improvement-notes.md.
